Switch Electric --- Homepage Redesign
Website Overview
Switch Electric LLC is a residential and commercial electrical
contractor based in Port Neches, Texas, serving customers across
Southeast Texas. Its services include electrical repairs and
installations, new construction, remodeling, generators, smart-home
solutions, and EV charger installation.

The redesigned homepage uses the creative concept "The Power Behind
Everything" to make the company's offering easier to understand, help
different customer groups find relevant services, and guide visitors
toward requesting an estimate.
Three Main Improvements
1. Clearer Value Proposition
The previous headline, "Powering Your Life When it Matters the Most,"
communicates the brand's promise but does not clearly explain the range
of electrical services.

The new hero makes Switch Electric's electrical services and residential
and commercial audience clearer from the first screen, while retaining
the established brand message as part of the brand identity.
2. More Direct Service Discovery
The redesign gives customers clearer paths to the services relevant to
their needs, including:
- Residential and commercial electrical services
- New construction and remodeling
- Generators and backup power
- EV charging
This replaces a less differentiated service-discovery experience with
more purposeful customer pathways.
3. Stronger Trust-to-Conversion Journey
The existing website includes useful trust signals, such as Google
reviews, credentials, and genuine project and team photography. The
redesign brings these elements into a more intentional relationship with
service discovery and estimate actions.

The primary estimate action continues to use the existing Jobber
destination. No replacement enquiry form or non-functional submission
flow has been introduced.
Design Improvements
- Visual direction: Applies the "The Power Behind Everything"
  concept while retaining the existing brand identity.
- Content hierarchy: Gives the main proposition, service pathways,
  credibility information, and estimate action clearer roles in the
  homepage.
- Layout and navigation: Organises services around customer needs
  to make relevant information easier to find.
- Trust and consistency: Uses the company's existing photography,
  logo, business information, reviews, and credentials as the basis
  for the presentation.
- Conversion experience: Keeps estimate actions connected to the
  existing Jobber destination.
Project Notes
The redesign is based on the supplied company research and existing
website information. Business details and service coverage should remain
consistent with verified source material.
